QUESTIONS

FREQUENTLY ASKED

What file formats do you provide?

+

We provide all major embroidery formats including DST, PES, JEF, EXP, XXX, VP3, HUS, VIP, and more. You'll receive files compatible with all embroidery machines.

How long does a Design take?

+

For our normal turnaround is 24 hours. Rush orders will be deliverd within 3-4 hours.

Do you offer free revisions?

+

Yes! All packages include free revisions. We'll work with you until you're 100% satisfied with the final result. Your happiness is our priority.

What's your pricing structure?

+

Pricing starts at $1.5 for simple designs. Complex designs and larger sizes are priced based on stitch count and complexity. Contact us for a custom quote.

Can you digitize any type of design?

+

Yes! We handle all types of designs including logos, text, complex artwork, 3D puff, applique, and specialty embroidery. No project is too complex.

Do you offer bulk discounts?

+

Absolutely! We offer volume discounts for multiple designs and monthly subscription packages for regular clients. Contact us for custom pricing.
